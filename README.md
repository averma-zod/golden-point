### ⚡ The Core Concept

1. **The Golden Point Card**  
   Grants the cardholder one irrevocable request of their choosing (travel, challenges, milestones). When presented, designated members **must** say yes.

2. **The ₹25,000 Fixed Deposit (FD)**  
   Every cardholder maintains a standing ₹25,000 security commitment in the vault. If a member refuses a valid request, their FD **unlocks immediately** into the redeemer's discretionary purse.

3. **The 3-Strike Rule for Newcomers**  
   Prospective members must participate in group plans at least **3 times** to become eligible contenders. Final induction requires a **100% unanimous vote** of the GP Committee.

4. **Mint Your Own Circle**  
   Any group of friends can create a custom circle with bespoke currency, FD stakes, committee founders, and generated scannable QR cards.
